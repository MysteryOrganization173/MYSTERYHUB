# Mystery Hub — Brand Guidelines

> Last updated: July 2026

## Positioning

**Mystery Hub** is a **digital services platform**.

It is **not** primarily an internet-packages website. Internet packages are **one service** within a broader ecosystem of digital and mobile services (connectivity, marketplace, wallet, results checking, earn/referrals, business/agent tools, and more).

**Tagline:** Everything Digital. One Trusted Place.

## Preferred language

Use these phrases in docs, metadata, and UI copy:

| Prefer | Instead of |
|---|---|
| Digital Services | “data site”, “data shop” |
| Mobile Services | narrow “airtime-only” framing |
| Internet Packages | “cheap data”, “data deals”, “Buy Data” as the brand |
| Digital Connectivity | discount-first slogans |

## Avoid

- Emphasizing **cheap data**, “best prices”, or bargain-first messaging as the brand story
- Framing the whole product as a data-bundle marketplace
- Legacy “Mystery Bundle Hub” / mystery-box marketplace wording in public docs
